Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

ARVENAIRE ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains the information processed when you use the website, submit an inquiry, request optional email delivery of a public resource, or purchase/book through a third-party platform.

1. Information you provide

  • Inquiry data: information you voluntarily submit, such as name, email address, optional phone number, current country, career stage, target role, selected topic and message.
  • Resource-request data: name, email address and the public resource you asked to receive/open through the optional email-delivery form.
  • Country-interest data: name, email address and selected future country route when you join a country-interest list.
  • Booking and payment data: paid products and consultation bookings are processed through Topmate. ARVENAIRE does not ask you to enter payment-card or bank credentials into its own website forms.

2. How website forms are processed

ARVENAIRE website forms are submitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service. The submitted fields and basic page context needed to route the inquiry are transmitted to that service so the request can be delivered and handled. Do not submit passwords, payment-card data, government ID numbers, medical information or other unnecessary sensitive information through these forms.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and requested guidance.
  • To record country-interest or resource-delivery requests you deliberately submit.
  • To support purchases, bookings and access issues.
  • To understand and improve ARVENAIRE's resources and website experience.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and protect the service from abuse.

4. Service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the requested feature, including Web3Forms for website form delivery and Topmate for checkout, payment and scheduling. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

5. Technical and analytics data

Hosting, security, browser and third-party service infrastructure may process ordinary technical information such as IP address, user agent, request timing or page context as part of delivering and protecting the service. If ARVENAIRE enables analytics, analytics events may also be used to understand aggregate website interaction. ARVENAIRE does not use this policy to claim collection of data that a feature does not actually need.

6. Data security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to reduce risk, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, support, record-keeping, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with you. You can make a privacy request through the Contact Page. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant record.

8. External links

ARVENAIRE links to official scholarship, university, employer and third-party platform websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those services, not by ARVENAIRE.
