Japan · MEXT research students

MEXT 2027 for Indian engineers: what the official cycle actually says.

The 2027 Research Student route is a strong option for graduate-level study in Japan, but the India embassy cycle has already closed. This guide separates confirmed 2027 facts from preparation advice for a future application.

Reviewed: 11 September 2026Official-source check: Embassy of Japan in India6 min read

Current status first

For the 2027 MEXT Research Student scholarship through the Embassy of Japan in India, the preliminary application deadline was 15 May 2026 at 11:59 p.m. The Embassy later listed the written examination for 5 July 2026 and interviews from 16 to 19 July 2026. If you are reading this in September 2026, that India embassy application window is closed.

Do not reuse the 2027 deadline for a future cycle. MEXT dates, forms and screening details can change. Always start from the Embassy of Japan in India page and the linked MEXT/Study in Japan guidelines for the exact year you are targeting.

What the 2027 India page confirms

The Research Student category is graduate-school level and includes master’s and doctoral pathways. For the India 2027 cycle, the Embassy page explicitly listed engineering fields including electrical engineering, electronics and communications, mechanical engineering, aerospace engineering, robotics, and computer science/information technology.

The same official page listed monthly stipends of ¥143,000 for preparatory/non-regular study, ¥144,000 for master’s or professional degree students, and ¥145,000 for doctoral students, with education fees exempted and round-trip airfare provided under the scholarship conditions. These figures are specific to the published 2027 research-student information; re-check them for later years.

Understand the selection sequence

The India embassy process begins with the preliminary application and document screening. Applicants who clear that stage move to the Embassy’s next screening steps. The exact sequence, examination requirements and documents must be read from the current application guidelines rather than inferred from old blogs or videos.

Research plan quality matters

For engineering applicants, the strongest preparation is not a generic “I like robotics” narrative. Build a research question that connects your previous evidence to a specific technical problem, method and expected contribution. A proposal around SLAM, robot perception, embedded control or manufacturing automation should explain the actual problem and evaluation method, not just list technologies.

Supervisor outreach comes later in the official process

The 2027 Embassy page links the official Letter of Provisional Acceptance material. Follow the current MEXT guidance on when applicants may contact universities and professors; do not mass-email faculty before understanding the year-specific rules and placement procedure.

How to prepare for the next cycle

  1. Build evidence now: research project, thesis, publication, technical report or reproducible engineering project.
  2. Create a research-plan draft: problem, prior work, method, experiments, expected result and fit with Japanese laboratories.
  3. Map potential laboratories: identify faculty whose recent work genuinely overlaps with your proposed topic.
  4. Prepare academic records: keep transcripts, degree documents and grading explanations organised.
  5. Watch only official sources for the next call: do not assume April/May dates until the new India notice is published.

What ARVENAIRE will not promise

No template, consultant or portfolio can guarantee a MEXT selection. The purpose of a good preparation system is to make your evidence clearer, your research proposal more coherent and your application less error-prone while the official selection remains entirely with the relevant Japanese authorities.
