This policy applies to ARVENAIRE's current paid Germany digital product and 1-on-1 strategy session. Any mandatory rights under applicable consumer law and any binding payment-platform requirements continue to apply.

Because this is a downloadable digital product containing editable files, templates, trackers and guides, purchases are generally treated as final once the buyer has been given access to the paid bundle, except where applicable law or the payment platform requires otherwise.

2. Germany Engineering Strategy Session — ₹2,499 / 120 minutes

  • Rescheduling: request rescheduling at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time through the available Topmate booking controls or support route.

3. Included Transition Kit

The ₹2,499 strategy session currently includes the Germany Transition Kit. If the paid digital kit has already been delivered before a session cancellation, any refund treatment may need to account for the delivered digital component, subject to applicable law and the checkout/platform terms shown to the buyer.

4. How to request support

For a purchase, access, cancellation or refund issue, use the Contact Page and include the email used for the booking or purchase plus enough non-sensitive information to identify the transaction. Do not send payment-card credentials.
