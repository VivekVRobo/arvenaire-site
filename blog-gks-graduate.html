GKS Graduate: choose the application track from the official guideline, not internet folklore.

Embassy Track and University Track are both official GKS routes. Neither should be described as automatically “easier.” The right choice depends on the current guideline, eligible institutions and your application strategy.

Reviewed: 11 September 2026Official-source check: Study in Korea / NIIED5 min read

What is current right now

The latest graduate-degree application guideline located on the official Study in Korea GKS notice board during this review is the 2026 GKS-G guideline, originally posted on 2 February 2026 and later revised in February. The 2026 cycle has already reached final-result stage.

A 2027 GKS Undergraduate guideline was published on 9 September 2026. That is a different programme. Do not copy undergraduate deadlines, university lists or procedures into a future graduate application.

For a future GKS-G application, wait for the graduate-degree notice for that exact cycle. The embassy, university list, application method and first-round deadlines are cycle-specific.

Embassy Track vs University Track

The official 2026 GKS-G notice states that applicants apply either through the Korean Embassy in their country of citizenship or directly to a designated university.

Embassy Track

This route begins with the relevant Korean diplomatic mission. The 2026 programme used the Study in Korea online application system for Embassy Track. Applicants should read the current country-specific embassy notice because first-round submission details and additional requirements can differ.

University Track

This route begins directly with a GKS-designated university. The current university list and eligible departments matter more than generic advice about which route is “less competitive.” A route with fewer institutions or a poor research fit can be worse for you even if someone online labels it easier.

Benefits and language training: verify the exact guideline

Study in Korea describes GKS degree-program benefits broadly as including airfare, language-training costs, tuition and study allowances. Exact amounts, exceptions and programme conditions belong to the cycle-specific guideline, so ARVENAIRE does not hard-code a stipend figure into this evergreen article.

Korean-language training and TOPIK-related rules can vary by programme and circumstance. Read the current GKS-G guideline before assuming every scholar follows an identical language-year path.

How to choose a route intelligently

  1. Start with eligible universities and departments. Your field and research fit come before route preference.
  2. Read your embassy notice. Country-specific first-round instructions are authoritative for Embassy Track.
  3. Compare route constraints. Look at how many universities you may choose, how documents are submitted and which programmes are open in that cycle.
  4. Build one coherent academic story. Your study plan, personal statement and evidence should support the same technical direction.
  5. Do not optimise for rumours about acceptance rate. Use official constraints and your actual fit.

For engineering applicants

A stronger application connects previous evidence to a realistic graduate objective. If your focus is robotics, embedded systems, automation or AI, show what you have already built, measured or researched and why the target department is the right environment for the next step. Avoid name-dropping major Korean companies unless there is a genuine academic or career connection.
