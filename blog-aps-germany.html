Germany · APS India

APS India: what it verifies, who generally needs it, and why timing is not fixed.

The old version of this article overstated APS as mandatory for every Indian student and quoted a fixed 4–8 week timeline. The official APS India guidance is more nuanced.

Reviewed: 11 September 2026Official-source check: APS India5 min read

What APS India actually does

APS India verifies academic documents and educational qualifications issued by Indian educational institutions for applicants intending to study in Germany. A successful APS certificate records the result of that verification. APS does not decide university admission and it does not decide visa applications.

APS India states that the certificate is generally required as part of the German student-visa documentation. That wording matters: applicants should not turn “generally required” into “mandatory for absolutely everyone.”

There are exemptions and different procedures

APS India lists situations in which an applicant may not need APS verification. Examples can include certain PhD or postdoctoral cases, relevant qualifications not issued by an Indian institution, some international school qualifications, and applicants covered by specific German or EU public-funding scholarship exemptions.

Use the APS requirements quiz and the checklist for your own educational pathway. Your nationality alone is not the decisive factor; APS India says the relevant issue is where the academic documents were issued and which procedure applies.

Do not plan around a promised processing time

APS India explicitly says processing time depends on application volume and on responses from schools, boards, universities and other institutions. It does not provide one fixed processing time for every individual case. That is why ARVENAIRE no longer publishes a generic “4–8 weeks” estimate as if it were an official service level.

The practical lesson is still to start early, but the correct reason is uncertainty. Build APS into your application timeline with buffer rather than treating an internet estimate as a deadline guarantee.

Document rules are procedure-specific

APS India currently instructs applicants to use the checklist that matches their procedure. Its FAQ says required documents are generally submitted by post or courier. It also states that documents already issued in English or bilingual format generally do not need notarisation; notarised English translations are required when documents are only in an Indian regional language and need translation.

This directly contradicts broad advice that “all APS documents must be notarised.” Follow the current checklist, not a copied checklist from another applicant.

What happens during verification

APS may need confirmation from the responsible educational institution through an official channel. APS India explains that a “non-support” status can mean the school, board or university has not provided the information required to complete verification. In that situation, applicants may need to ask their institution to respond through an official institutional channel.

A practical APS workflow

  1. Use the official APS requirements quiz to identify the correct procedure.
  2. Read the matching current checklist in full before paying or posting anything.
  3. Prepare legible copies and translations exactly as the checklist requires.
  4. Apply with enough schedule buffer for institutional verification delays.
  5. Track the application through the APS portal and respond promptly if APS requests more information.
  6. After issuance, keep the digitally signed APS PDF unchanged when submitting it to relevant institutions.

Keep APS separate from admission and visa promises

An APS certificate is an academic-verification document. It does not guarantee university admission, and APS India expressly says it does not guarantee that a visa will be issued. Treat admission, APS and visa processing as separate gates in your Germany plan.
